Welcome to Vertex Vihanta

Vertex Vihanta is a prestigious development that offers premium villas in Varthur. A well-desired residential area along the eastern edge of Bangalore City. Presented by Vertex Infras this is no ordinary gated community, it's entirely an upscale project that is composed of large, spacious villas.

These individual houses are configured as 4 BHK units and have a lavish unit size extending 2200 Sq.Ft. All the houses are styled similarly in this community with ultra-modern architecture.

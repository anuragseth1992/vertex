Welcome to Vertex Wellington

Vertex Infras has lauched Vertex Wellington in Gunjur, Bangalore. A residential project spread over 0.42 Acres , it offers ample amount of facilities for residents. The project was launched in July 2023. It offers Under Construction units. Popular configurations include 2 BHK, 3 BHK units. As per the area plan, units are in the size range of 603.0 - 1238.0 sq.ft.. There are 32 units in Vertex Wellington. Overall, there is 1 building.
